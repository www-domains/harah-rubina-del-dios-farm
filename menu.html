Our Story

Our Culinary Journey

At Harah Rubina Del Dios Farm, we take pride in serving authentic Filipino cuisine made with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients. Our menu reflects the rich culinary heritage of Bukidnon while offering modern twists on classic favorites.

Every dish tells a story of tradition, family, and the bountiful harvest of our region. From our farm-fresh vegetables to our signature coffee, each item on our menu is carefully prepared to bring you the authentic taste of Bukidnon.

Menu Highlights

What Makes Our Menu Special

Experience the best of Filipino cuisine with our carefully curated selection

Signature Coffee

Indulge in our freshly brewed Bukidnon coffee, made from locally sourced beans. Each cup is a celebration of our region's rich coffee heritage, served hot or iced to your preference.

Authentic Filipino Dishes

From classic Adobo to comforting Sinigang, our main dishes are prepared using traditional family recipes passed down through generations, ensuring authentic flavors in every bite.

Sweet Treats

End your meal with our delightful desserts like Leche Flan and Halo-Halo. Each dessert is made fresh daily, using the finest ingredients to satisfy your sweet cravings.

Fresh Snacks

Enjoy our crispy Lumpia and sweet Turon, perfect for sharing or as a quick bite. All snacks are made fresh to order, ensuring maximum flavor and crunch.

Family Sets

Perfect for groups! Our family set meals offer great value with a variety of dishes that can feed 4-6 people. Ideal for celebrations, reunions, or family gatherings.

Custom Orders

Have special dietary needs or preferences? We're happy to accommodate custom orders. Just let us know in advance, and we'll prepare something special just for you.

About Us

Harah Rubina Del Dios Farm, owned by Luisita Oponda, is a premier destination in Santo Niño Purok II, Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon, offering diverse recreational activities and authentic farm experiences.
